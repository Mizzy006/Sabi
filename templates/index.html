How it works

Four steps from choosing a role to a record employers can check.

  1. 1

    Pick a role and plan

    Choose the job you are aiming for. The planner fits a study path to your hours and data budget.

  2. 2

    Build the model

    Download the starter workbook for your role and build the model in Excel within 90 minutes.

  3. 3

    Get it stress-tested

    Upload your workbook. Sabi changes six business conditions, like the exchange rate, and checks your model still holds.

  4. 4

    Defend and get verified

    Answer a few questions about your own formulas. Pass, and you receive a signed record employers can check.

Results employers can check

Every evidence brief is signed. Anyone with its link or QR code can confirm it has not been altered.

  • Signed and tamper-evidentChange a single field and verification fails.
  • Blind by defaultHiring teams see skills first, not names or universities.
